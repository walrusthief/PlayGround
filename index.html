📖 How to Play

Move: tap ground to walk · or drag left joystick. Pinch-free auto camera.

Interact: tap glowing NPC / enemy / loot. Selected hero shows ring.

Combat (RTwP): real-time with pause — like BG1. 5e math underneath: d20 + prof + mod vs AC, crits, spell slots, death saves.

Actions: bottom bar — Attack, Spells, Potion, Second Wind, Hide, Rest, Pause.

Party: tap portraits to select. AI companions auto-attack.

Rests: Short Rest spends Hit Dice · Long Rest needs inn/campfire.

Quests: follow the Journal (📜). Talk to everyone with ! or ?

Save: auto-saves; Continue from title.
